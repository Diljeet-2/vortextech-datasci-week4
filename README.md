# 📊 End-to-End Customer Churn Analysis Report

A professional end-to-end data analytics project completed as part of the **Data Science & Analytics Internship Track – Week 4 Capstone Project**. This project analyzes customer churn data to identify the key factors influencing customer retention and provides actionable business recommendations through data cleaning, exploratory data analysis (EDA), and interactive visualizations.

---

## 📌 Project Objective

The primary objective of this project is to answer the following business question:

> **Why are customers leaving the company, and what actions can help reduce customer churn?**

By analyzing historical customer data, this project uncovers meaningful insights that can support better business decisions and improve customer retention strategies.

## 🔍 Key Findings

* Customers with **month-to-month contracts** are significantly more likely to churn.
* Customers with **higher monthly charges** have a greater probability of leaving.
* **New customers** (shorter tenure) are more likely to churn than long-term customers.
* Customers using **electronic check** as their payment method exhibit higher churn rates.

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

* Encourage customers to switch to **long-term contracts** by offering attractive discounts or loyalty benefits.
* Launch targeted **customer retention campaigns** for new customers during their first few months.
* Promote **automatic payment methods** through incentives to improve customer retention.
